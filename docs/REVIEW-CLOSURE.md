## Minimal user real-device trial

1. Install the exact APK identified in its report. Use phone speaker or wired output, mount the phone on a stand, and keep AR off initially.
2. Pair/list one original Joy-Con, export capability report and an IMU capture while doing six faces and three-axis turns. Repeat for four physical IDs. Buttons alone are not IMU proof; use the desktop v2 bridge if the phone driver lacks motion sensors.
3. Assign roles. Still-calibrate, recenter, bind hand directions for the final arrangement. Capture left-foot flat/closed and full-open. Tune right-foot axis/sign to the mounting.
4. Check hand single/rebound/alternating strokes, left-foot gradual half/open/slow-close/fast-close, and right-foot lift-only versus downstroke. Record counts; do not mark simulated traces as hardware passes.
5. Ring an open hat, disconnect the left foot: tail stops, no foot CHICK is synthesized. Reconnect/restart/background and verify NEEDS_RECENTER blocks motion until recentered; re-capture foot points.
6. Enable AR on a supported phone. Reposition/rotate/scale the kit, then recenter and rebind. Play all four limbs for at least 30 minutes; export source frames and audio trace. Acoustic latency needs a shared physical trigger/microphone time reference, not only application timestamps.

The eventual acceptance record must identify phone/OS/controller IDs and mounting, transport, audio output, AR on/off, exact source/APK/certificate, counters and uncertainty. No hardware evidence is supplied by this software delivery.
